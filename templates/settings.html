How the score is calculated

BacklogVision normalizes the weighted ratings to a 0–100 priority score.

Each rating is multiplied by its configured weight. The weighted rating is then normalized. Score = ((Weighted Rating − 1) ÷ 9) × 100

When the weights total 100%, a work item rated 1 across every criterion scores 0, while an item rated 10 across every criterion scores 100.

Criterion Guide

↗ Business Value

Revenue potential, operational efficiency, or measurable business benefit.

◷ Urgency

Deadlines, time-sensitive needs, and consequences of delaying the work.

♡ Customer Impact

Improvements to usability, satisfaction, accessibility, or customer outcomes.

◇ Risk Reduction

Security, reliability, compliance, technical uncertainty, or other risk mitigation.

◎ Strategic Alignment

Contribution to product direction and longer-term organizational goals.

Configuration Tips ✦

• Use higher weights for your most important business drivers.

• Keep the same rating scale across all backlog items.

• Review weights when business objectives change.

• Compare the resulting ranking with team judgment before finalizing a sprint.

What happens next?

Once you save valid weights, the dashboard can calculate priority scores for backlog items and use them during sprint planning.
